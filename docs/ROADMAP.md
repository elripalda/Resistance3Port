# Roadmap

What's planned after the 0.2.0 beta, roughly in priority order. Nothing here has a date; priorities depend on what testers report.

## Stability

- Find and fix the remaining causes of the occasional freezes and crashes.
- Test levels past the opening level, using testers' reports and logs.

## Visual quality

- Make ambient occlusion correct with DLSS, so it can be turned back on.
- Make DLSS less experimental: fix the remaining texture shimmer and dark patches.

## Performance

- Reduce slowdowns when aiming down sights and in heavy fights: smarter scheduling of the depth-of-field SPU jobs.
- Reduce the remaining microstutter.

## Features

- Ultrawide (21:9 / 32:9) support.
- Online play through community servers (re-psn / Horizon).
- Possibly one launcher for all three Resistance ports.
